            What this is

            Komyut is a free commuter guide for Metro Cebu: jeepney codes, bus terminals and routes, popular places with sakay directions, and fare estimates. It has no accounts, ads, cookies or analytics. Your browser does fetch the map library from cdnjs and map tiles from OpenStreetMap.

            How much to trust it

            • Verified (multiple sources) Endpoints agree across two or more independent sources. This is not an official LTFRB check.
            • Community-reported Listed by one source, or the details are partly inferred.
            • Unverified – help us confirm The route or pin needs confirmation.

            Route lines are hand-placed estimates, not surveyed. Fares are estimates; the fare guide posted inside the vehicle settles any difference.

            Suggest a correction

            Spotted a wrong code, route, fare or pin? Open a “Route/Fare correction” issue on GitHub and include a source or the date you rode it. The project lives at github.com/Splashzzz/Komyut.
